Other business-insight ideas (non-table additions)

  • Streak counter per metric — "FAD declining 3 days in a row" → triggers attention.
  • Percentile ranking — "Today's Overall FAD is in the 25th percentile of the last 30 days" → tells you HOW bad/good without needing context.
  • Anomaly flag — when Day value is >1.5× standard deviation from 30d mean → ⚠ amber label.
  • Top-3 movers strip — biggest improvers / decliners by absolute pp — surfaces the actionable items.
  • "Why" hints — when Reschedule rate spikes >1.5× 7d-avg, auto-show top reschedule reasons inline (we already store these).
  • Goal lines — once we agree thresholds (e.g. Overall FAD ≥ 80%), color the day cell red if below target — actionable beyond comparison.
  • City callout — "Mumbai dragging Overall FAD down by 6pp" — automatically detect which city is responsible for the day's overall miss.
Recommendation

Go with Option B (verdict pill) + Founder summary banner at the very top + Insight strip per section. Skip sparklines for v1 — they look great but are extra render cost on a 28MB file, and verdict pill + heat cell already give 90% of the glance value.

Concretely the page becomes:

  1. Founder summary banner — 4 stat blocks (vs yesterday / vs 7d / vs 30d / bottom-line verdict)
  2. City × Category Leaders (untouched per your direction)
  3. KPI Snapshot — insight strip + 9-column table (FAD by JT + FSD by JT)
  4. Trip Efficiency — insight strip + 9-column table (city, trip metrics, JT)
  5. TAT Comparison — insight strip + 9-column table (city, overall, JT)
